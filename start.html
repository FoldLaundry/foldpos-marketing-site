Before you begin

Have these ready.

About an hour in all. Stop whenever you like: your ticks stay on this device.

  • The computer by your register, with Chrome. Any Windows PC or Mac from the last 5 years.
  • Your receipt printer and its USB cable.
  • Your tag printer and barcode scanner, if you have them.
  • Any scale. You weigh the bag and type the pounds.
  • Your phone, charged. You film your floor with it.
  • Your price list, or an export from Cents or CleanCloud.
  • Your staff’s names.

Setup

Six steps to your first order.

  1. Account created

Account created. You did that when you signed up.

Step 1 of 6 · 5 minutes

Set up the counter.

Open pos.foldpos.com in Chrome on the computer by your register, and bookmark it. Most shops already own the rest.

For tags, add a tag printer and a barcode scanner.

Any scale works: weigh the bag and type the pounds.

Most store owners use Windows. Chrome gives the easiest printing.

See the hardware guide

Step 2 of 6 · 15 minutes

Add your prices.

Settings › Import data

Export your customers and prices from Cents, CleanCloud or a spreadsheet, then pick the file. No list? Start from ours.

Price by the pound with a minimum, by the bag or by the piece.

Every garment becomes a button at the counter.

Check what came in. You can undo an import.

Open Fold POSOr have us do it

Step 3 of 6 · 10 minutes

Connect your printer.

Settings › Receipts › Connect printer

Plug the receipt printer into the counter computer by USB. In Chrome, pick it from the list and print a test page.

On Windows with Edge or Firefox, or if the list is empty, pair the free print helper. On a Mac, use Chrome.

Tag printer: run Calibrate cutter once in Settings › Receipts. Each piece gets its own tag: 1/3, 2/3, 3/3.

No need to add the printer in Windows or macOS first.

No tag printer yet? Start with receipts and add tags when you’re ready.

Step 4 of 7 · Optional

Map your machines.

Laundromat

Open Laundromat in Fold POS and point your phone at the code on the screen. Film one slow walk down each aisle, check the floor Fold draws, then scan each machine. Your floor becomes a live map: what’s free, what’s running and how long is left.

About 10 to 20 minutes. Fold stickers come in the mail for machines with no barcode.

Later is fine. The counter works without it.

Step 4 of 6 · 10 minutes · Optional

Invite your staff.

Employees, in the left menu

Give each person their own login and a 4-digit code. Between customers, a code unlocks the counter as that person, so every order shows who took it.

Staff check orders in and out. Only you change prices and settings.

Meet Hey Fold

Worried about…?

Most worries have a short answer.

What if the internet drops?

The counter keeps taking orders and sends them when it’s back.

Do I need new hardware?

Most shops already own what Fold POS needs. It works with Epson, Star, Bixolon and Zebra printers, impact or thermal, USB or network.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC.”

That appears because the print helper is new. Click More info, then Run anyway. The helper only talks to Fold POS and your printers.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Pick a plan in Settings › Plan & billing. If you don’t, the POS goes read-only. Everything you entered stays.
