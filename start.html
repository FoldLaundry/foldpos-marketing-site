Open on Fold POS today.

Seven short steps from sign-up to your first real order. Watch each one, then do it.

Start the tour
About an hour in all · 0 of 7 done

Step 1 of 7

Create your account.

Your name, your shop and what it does. Start typing the address and pick it from the list: street, city, state and ZIP fill themselves.

Sign up2 minutes

Step 2 of 7

Check what you already have.

A computer with Chrome, a receipt printer, a cash drawer and your card terminal. Most shops own all four. Dry cleaners add a tag printer and a scanner.

See the hardware guide5 minutes

Step 3 of 7

Bring your price list over.

Import it from Cents, CleanCloud or a spreadsheet in Settings › Import data. Every garment becomes a button at the counter.

Or have us do it15 minutes

Step 4 of 7

Connect your printers.

Plug in the receipt printer and print a test. Tag printers cut one 1 × 3 in tag per piece. On Windows, Edge, Firefox or Safari, the free print helper does the connecting.

Get the print helper10 minutes

Step 5 of 7

Add your staff.

Everyone gets their own login and a 4-digit code. Between customers, a code unlocks the counter as that person, so every order shows who took it.

10 minutes

Step 6 of 7

Let customers know it's ready.

Turn on customer texts in Settings › Customer texts. Customers hear the moment their order is ready, and can book a pickup by text.

5 minutes

Step 7 of 7

Take your first real order.

Tap the garments, take payment, and the ticket and tags print. That's a normal day from here on.

Open the POS5 minutes
