Welcome to Fold POS

Here is everything to get your shop running, in order. Most stores are taking real orders the same day; the times are rough guides.

Your first week

  1. Create your account2 min

    Sign up at pos.foldpos.com/signup. Pick everything your shop does (laundromat, wash & fold, dry cleaning, alterations); the POS shows you only what fits.

  2. Check your hardware5 min

    Most shops already have what they need. The hardware guide lists what each kind of shop adds and where to buy it.

  3. Set up your store20 min

    The seven-step setup guide walks through the browser, your price list, the printer, staff, customer texts and your first order.

  4. Connect your printers10 min

    Receipt and tag printers work through Chrome, or through the free Fold print helper on Windows and other browsers. See printers that work.

  5. Bring your data over15 min

    Import your price list and customers in Settings › Import data, from Cents, CleanCloud or a spreadsheet. Rather we do it? Email us the export and we’ll import it for you.

  6. Add your staff10 min

    Add each employee in Employees with their own login, and give each a 4-digit code so they can unlock the counter between customers.

  7. Turn on customer texts5 min

    Customers get a text when their order is ready, and can book pickups by text or online. Set it up in Settings › Customer texts.

  8. Take your first real order5 min

    Check in a garment, print the ticket and tags, and check it out. The first-login tour shows you around the screens.

For your kind of shop

Do these once the basics are in place. Skip what doesn’t apply.

Dry cleaners

  • Set up the tag printer and run Calibrate cutter once (Settings › Receipts).
  • Every order has a Pieces view; Find a piece on Orders finds any tag.

Laundromats

  • Add your machines and lay out the floor in Machine management.
  • Connect your machine payment system if you use one.

Plants and drop stores

  • Link your shops in Settings › Plant & drop stores, and set the plant’s rates.
  • See how bags move: Follow the bag.
  • Drivers install the Fold POS Driver app on their phone.

Pickup & delivery

  • Set your service area and pickup windows.
  • Read how delivery works; drivers use the Fold POS Driver app.
