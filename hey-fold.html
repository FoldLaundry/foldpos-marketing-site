The counter that listens.

How it works

Three steps, at the counter or anywhere in the POS.

  1. Call itPress Caps Lock on the computer, tap the orb, or say its name.
  2. Say the orderThe customer, the pieces and the day: “two shirts and a suit for Maria, ready Thursday.”
  3. Say “yes”Fold reads it back with the price. On your “yes”, the ticket and the tags print.

Say it the way you would say it

A few orders, and what lands on the ticket.

You sayOn the ticket
“Two bags and a comforter, rush it.”Wash & fold by the pound for the two bags, the comforter, and the rush charge. One tag per piece.
“Five shirts and a suit for Maria, ready Saturday.”Maria’s ticket: five shirts and a two-piece suit, ready Saturday.
“Hem two pairs of pants.”Two hems, as alterations, with the usual turnaround for alterations.
“Dos bolsas y un edredón, urgente.”The same order as the first one, said in Spanish.

Prices come from your own price list.

Hey Fold questions

What is Hey Fold?

Fold’s voice. Say the customer, the pieces and when it’s due, and the order is typed, priced and printing. Fold reads it back and waits for your “yes” before anything happens.

How do I talk to it?

On a computer, press Caps Lock to talk and press it again when you’re done, or tap the orb. It works on every screen.

Is it listening all the time?

No. It stays quiet until you call its name, press Caps Lock or tap the orb.

Does it cost extra?

No. Hey Fold is on every plan, in English and Spanish.
