Good to know

  • Impact printers can’t print barcodes. Fine for garment tags. For bag labels use a thermal printer so the driver’s camera can scan them.
  • Don’t add the printer in macOS or Windows printer settings unless the setup guide says to. Chrome, or the Fold print helper, talks to it directly.
  • Chrome is the easiest browser. On Windows, Edge and Firefox work too, with the free print helper at foldpos.com/download.
  • No scale integration yet. For wash & fold, weigh on any scale and type the pounds.
  • Setting up? The seven-step guide at foldpos.com/setup walks through account, printer, staff and a first order.
