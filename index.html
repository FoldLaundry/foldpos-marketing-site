How an order actually moves

Four screens. One order, start to finish.

This is the same board your counter staff sees today — not a mockup of what a POS could look like.

01Take the order

Ring up an order the way your counter actually works

Tap through wash-and-fold, dry cleaning, or alterations, add bags or per-pound pricing, toggle express service, and apply a discount — then take payment by cash, card, or pay-on-delivery, all from one screen.

02Track every stage

See every order's exact stage, all day long

Orders move across your real workflow — to-do, washing, drying, ironing, folding, completed — so nothing sits forgotten in a machine. Assign a washer or dryer in one tap and watch load progress in real time.

03Keep the details on the ticket

Every note, photo, and price change stays with the order

Log a broken zipper, snap a photo of a stain before you clean it, and see the full price breakdown by task — so whoever hands the order back knows exactly what happened to it.

04Run the shift

Close out the day without reopening a spreadsheet

Clock in and out, track revenue and order volume by service line, and hand off a clean shift log and activity feed to whoever opens tomorrow.

Beyond the counter

Before you switch

Questions owners actually ask

Do I need new hardware, or can I use what I have?

Fold POS runs on most iPads, Android tablets, and receipt printers you already own. If you'd rather not source your own, we sell ready-to-go counter bundles.

Can I bring over my orders and customers from Cents or CleanCloud?

Yes. Our migration tool imports customers, order history, and pricing so you're not starting from a blank counter on day one.

Is there a contract?

No. Every plan is month-to-month, and you can change tiers or cancel any time from your account settings.

Does it work across multiple locations?

Yes. Growth and Pro plans roll up reporting across locations while keeping each shop's own workflow board separate.

How is Fold POS related to Fold?

Fold POS is a standalone product built by the Fold team, purpose-built for laundromats and dry cleaners rather than general service businesses.
