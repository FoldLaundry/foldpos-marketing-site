    Questions

    Before you ask.

    Which printers does it work with?
    The receipt printer and tag printer you already own: Epson, Star, Bixolon and Zebra, impact or thermal, USB or network. Receipts print the moment an order is placed, with one 1 × 3 in tag per piece, from a heat-seal tag printer or your impact printer. See every printer.

    Does it run on Windows, a Mac and an iPad?
    Yes. Open pos.foldpos.com in any browser, or use the desktop app on the counter PC or the iPad by the register. Windows counter PCs get a small print helper from foldpos.com/download.
    Can I bring my customers from Cents or CleanCloud?
    Yes, from Cents, CleanCloud or a CSV. You can take your data with you the same way.
    Can I use my own card processor?
    Yes. Card, cash, check, store credit and split payments, on your processor at your rates.
    Is it in Spanish?
    The counter, Hey Fold and the texts your customers get all work in English and Spanish.
    Do I need a card to start?
    No. 14 days free, no card, month to month. Change or cancel from Settings.
